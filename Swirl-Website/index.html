If I were to explain track and field to an 8th grader, I would say that track and field is a sport where you run competitively. Although it may sound simple at first, many people switch from their main sport to track in hopes of “making it big,” but it's an Olympic-level sport for a reason.

A unique tid bit about Track and Field is: Every event uses a different kind of athletic skill, so sprinters, distances runners,jumpers, and throwers don't train the same way, yet all come together under one sport.
